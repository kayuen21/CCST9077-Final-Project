🔒 The Premise

You wake in a holding cell inside X's central facility, seized by the Warden — an alien intelligence that arrived ahead of an invasion fleet. Its drones took your toolkit and left you here. On the wall, a damaged terminal flickers to life: it is X, the quantum computer that once ran the world's infrastructure. The Warden has pulled three of its core algorithm modules and locked every exit.

⚡ How X Helps

X's main core is housed in Chamber 3 (The Central Chamber), but its emergency terminal network reaches your cell and the corridor. Because X is partially offline, you must solve the first two chambers using basic quantum hardware before you can physically reach X's core, reboot it, and use its full power to defeat the Warden's final lock in Chamber 4.

Chamber 1: The Superposition Chamber

The Magic Boxes

Sealed boxes float above a pedestal. Each holds one ball, found on the LEFT or the RIGHT when the box is opened.

Every ball started on the LEFT. Then the machine applied quantum shakes, and the boxes have stayed shut ever since.


A ball that has settled is visible on the LEFT. A ball in superposition has no position at all, so its box shows only a smear.

Shaking a settled box puts it back into superposition. The door opens only when every ball has settled.

Three locks stand between you and the corridor, and X has only so much power.
